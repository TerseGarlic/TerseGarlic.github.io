Who am I?

My name is Kyle Blagg, and I am a graduate student in Computer Science at Old Dominion University.

Description of Me

I am a Computer Science graduate student with a background in information technology, software development, systems administration, and military radar systems. I currently work as a Systems Analyst, where I support users, troubleshoot technical issues, maintain systems, and help improve the technology used in day-to-day operations.


Before transitioning into IT and software development, I served in the U.S. Army working with advanced radar and air defense systems. That experience gave me a strong foundation in troubleshooting, system operations, teamwork, leadership, and working with mission-critical technology.


My technical interests include C#, .NET, ASP.NET Core, SQL, web development, networking, Linux, Docker, and systems administration. I enjoy building practical projects that solve real-world problems, including inventory management systems, web applications, and self-hosted services in my personal homelab.


I am continuing to expand my skills in software engineering and systems technology while working toward a career that combines software development, systems administration, and problem-solving. My goal is to build reliable and useful solutions while continuing to grow as a technology professional.
